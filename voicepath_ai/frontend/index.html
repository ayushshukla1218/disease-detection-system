How It Works

01
🎙

Voice Recording

Upload or record a voice sample (30–120 seconds recommended). Recordings are processed locally and not stored.

02
🔬

Feature Extraction

41 acoustic features extracted: pitch statistics, 13 MFCCs (mean+std), ZCR, RMS energy, spectral centroid, bandwidth, and rolloff.

03
🤖

ML Classification

Trained Random Forest, SVM, and Gradient Boosting models compare results. Best model by F1-score on held-out subjects is used.

04
📊

Result + Confidence

Classification result shown with probability confidence. Results with <60% confidence are flagged as uncertain.
